**What I looked for**
- A sentence that tells what the act or line before it just showed: the gesture, then the gloss.
- Narration that says the same thing as the line of dialogue next to it, before or after.
- A recap of an earlier chapter that does no new work in this scene. This usually happens when the next chapter retells the last one from the other side.
- Callbacks told so many times they've stopped landing: the promise at the sink, the railing, "presenting," the rock and the hood.
- Then-and-now pairings, and tallies of what someone didn't do.

**What I left alone on purpose**
- Attraction said outright. That's your rule for the genre.
- The lie a character tells herself or himself ("I told myself a quarter to nine was good news").
- Deductions that give the reader something new: Cal working out that she checked his boxes, Jo seeing that the boat trip was her father's look at Cal.
- Cal's long case for the wire in 28 and 30. That's his belief at work, and the case he makes to himself is the point.
- Lines you wrote or approved on October 18 and 19. Where I raise one again, it's marked *Your call* with the new reason.

**Tags**
- **Fact:** an error on the page. Fix it whatever else you decide.
- **Strong:** a clear improvement.
- **Moderate:** an improvement, but smaller.
- **Minor:** a small trim.
- **Your call:** you kept it before, or the genre may want it said.
