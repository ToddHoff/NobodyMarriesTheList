## The look
- **Match the cover:** flat, hand-painted gouache with a soft screen-print grain, in the cover's mid-century palette of burnt orange, deep teal, navy, warm walnut and cream.
- **Each card lies where the scene puts it,** seen from above, filling most of a landscape frame. The props come from that moment in the book, sit cropped at the edges, and never cover the words.
- **A hand or a trace of a person** is in every image: his finger squaring the card, her pencil, a smudge of graphite. That's the romance. The card is never just paper on a table.
- **The card itself is what the book says it is:** a plain white 3 by 5 unlined index card from Janice's box (ch 30). Its corners go soft over the summer (ch 34).
- **Grayscale-safe:** dark ink on a clean card, no glare across the writing. It has to read on a Kindle Paperwhite.
- **No words but the card's:** no titles, logos, signatures or watermarks.
- **Size:** 1536 by 1024, landscape.

## Whose hand
- **Janice:** neat, small, upright print in blue ballpoint. She crosses her sevens (ch 1, ch 28).
- **Cal:** careful small square printing in blue ink. His sevens go straight down (epilogue).
- **Jo:** flat red carpenter's pencil, gray graphite, square capitals "the kind people use on boxes" (ch 2). Her strike lines are heavy and straight.
- **Owen:** not yet drawn. Cal's pufferfish in ch 36 has to look like a sea urchin.
