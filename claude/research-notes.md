## Jo's trade runs on a state tariff, a transfer station and a three-day job

### The paperwork on the truck door

California household movers have answered to the **Bureau of Household Goods and Services** since July 1, 2018, when Senate Bill 19 moved them out of the Public Utilities Commission ([CPUC](https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/news-and-updates/all-news/transfer-of-authority-of-household-goods-private-carriers-vessels-for-hire-commercial-air-operators)). The credential is the Household Movers Permit, numbered **"CAL-T" plus seven digits**, and the number must appear on advertisements, vehicles and contracts ([GoShare permit copy](https://goshare.co/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Official-Household-Movers-Permit-from-Bureau-of-Household-Goods-and-Services_California.pdf); [Yembo](https://yembo.ai/moving/estimates/intrastate/ca)). A firm established in 1987 spent its first 31 years under the PUC, so Frank would still say "PUC" and the Cal-T number sits under the family name on the cab doors. ## The payoff is easy to make and impossible to undo

### The secret payoff, step by step

"Most financial institutions allow other people to pay off your debt"; the payer needs the account number ([Experian](https://www.experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/can-someone-else-pay-off-my-debt/)). A lender's own explainer lists what is needed (lender, loan number, property address, homeowner's name, payoff amount) and notes that an anonymous payment is possible by cashier's check with no return address ([Quicken Loans](https://www.quickenloans.com/learn/can-you-pay-off-someone-elses-mortgage)). A son paid off his parents' mortgage "without their knowledge" in a documented 2014 case ([FindLaw](https://www.findlaw.com/legalblogs/law-and-life/legal-how-to-paying-off-someone-elses-mortgage/)). The obstacle is the exact figure. California limits payoff demand statements to an "entitled person" or an agent with the borrower's written authorization ([Civil Code 2943](https://law.onecle.com/california/civil/2943.html)), and one large servicer says flatly that "only UWM customers and authorized third parties may request a payoff quote" ([UWM](https://www.myuwmloan.com/help-center/payments/requesting-payoff-quote)). A monthly statement must show the outstanding principal balance, the interest rate and recent transactions ([CFPB Reg Z 1026.41](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/41/)), so the page on Jo's counter gives Cal the balance and, on the plan's premise, the loan number, but not the per-day interest that makes a payoff figure. The contrast with 2012 is useful: with his mother he had access and standing; with Jo he had neither.

What Jo then receives, in order: a zero balance online the same or next day (researcher's inference); a **paid-in-full letter within about two weeks**; an **escrow refund check within 20 business days** at the federal outer limit; and a full reconveyance recorded with the San Mateo County Recorder within about 51 days, the statute allowing 30 days for the lender and 21 for the trustee ([Navy Federal](https://www.navyfederal.org/loans-cards/mortgage/homeowner-resources/mortgage-payoff-process.html); [Reg X](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1024/34/); [Civil Code 2941](https://law.onecle.com/california/civil/2941.html)). Her impound account closes, so property tax and insurance bills now come to her directly. Any extra principal she had been paying shows as its own line on her statements, a paper record of her own effort that the payoff erases.

### Gift tax and the impossibility of refusing

Paying someone's debt is a gift from the payer. For 2026 the annual exclusion is **$19,000** and the lifetime exclusion **$15,000,000** ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/frequently-asked-questions-on-gift-taxes); [Bowditch & Dewey](https://www.bowditch.com/estateandtaxplanningblog/2025/10/28/big-increase-in-2026-federal-estate-exemption-irs-issues-2026-inflation-adjustments/)). On the research's working figure of $430,000 (the plan states no balance), the taxable gift is $411,000, Cal files **Form 709 by April 15, 2027**, pays nothing, and Jo owes nothing and files nothing ([IRS Form 709 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i709)). The gift is secret from her and declared to the IRS, with her name and address on a form he signs under penalty of perjury. He cannot take it back: "A gift, other than a gift in view of impending death, cannot be revoked by the giver" ([Civil Code 1148](https://law.onecle.com/california/civil/1148.html)). Her formal route is a written disclaimer within nine months and before accepting any benefit ([26 CFR 25.2518-2](https://www.govregs.com/regulations/title26_chapterI-i15_part25_subjgrp14_section25.2518-2)); the researcher's reading is that living in a house with a released lien looks like acceptance, so her only real remedy is the one in the plot. For 2012 the figures were $13,000 and $5,120,000 ([IRS 2012 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i709--2012.pdf)), and Stockton filed for bankruptcy on June 28 of that year ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockton,_California)).

### Jo's note

A personal note needs principal, a rate, a payment schedule, a maturity date and the maker's signature. The floor is the applicable federal rate, **4.87 percent for a loan over nine years made in June 2026**, as reported by an adviser, and the rate changes monthly ([Atlatl Advisers](https://atlatladvisers.com/insights/intra-family-loans-afr)). The ceiling is California's **10 percent usury cap** on loans between individuals ([C&H Law](https://www.ch-law.com/blog/2025/09/understanding-usury/)). Below the federal rate, the missing interest is treated as a gift from lender to borrower. The researcher's arithmetic on $430,000 at 4.87 percent is about $2,274 a month over 30 years, $2,807 over 20 and $3,371 over 15; at zero interest, $1,792 over 20. Two things here are inference and worth a lawyer's eye. First, if the payoff stays a gift and she simply sends money, her payments are gifts to him, and above $19,000 a year (about $1,583 a month) she is the one filing Form 709. Second, nothing compels Cal to cash her checks, so the monthly image of a check that does or does not clear is legally sound.

### The NDA

Reported relationship NDAs run from two pages to seventeen and bind only the non-famous party. Charlie Sheen's defined confidential information to include texts, photographs and recordings, set **$100,000 liquidated damages per breach**, assigned copyright in anything the signer created, and required confidential arbitration under JAMS rules ([Law Insider](https://www.lawinsider.com/resources/contract-teardown/establishing-trust-with-a-celebrity-nda)). The Stormy Daniels agreement ran 17 pages with $1 million per breach ([BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/hallielieberman/celebrity-gossip-sex-scandal-ndas)). One signer was never given a copy, so Jo insisting on a countersigned one is a true beat. A lawyer called the Combs agreement "the broadest non-disclosure agreement that I have ever seen" ([Miami Herald via AOL](https://www.aol.com/culture-silence-lawyer-calls-diddy-021031258.html)). The limits that actually reach a dating NDA are Civil Code 1670.11, which voids any waiver of the right to testify about crimes or sexual harassment, and the federal Speak Out Act ([Civil Code 1670.11](https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/civil-code/civ-sect-1670-11/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speak_Out_Act)). The "Silenced No More Act" is an employment law, so a character who cites it at Cal is slightly wrong, and a lawyer like Dawn would know the difference. A mutual two-page version would bind both, define confidential information narrowly, carve out lawyers, doctors, crime reports and subpoenas, set a term, drop the liquidated damages and keep disputes in California courts (researcher's assembly from the examples).

### Darlene's house

With a living trust "the successor trustee named in the trust controls it" and the house can be listed within weeks; in probate nobody can act until letters issue, and formal probate runs nine to eighteen months ([Ridley Law Offices](https://ridleylawoffices.com/inherited-house-california/); [Opendoor](https://www.opendoor.com/articles/how-long-does-it-take-to-sell-an-inherited-house)). For deaths on or after April 1, 2025 a primary residence worth **$750,000 or less** can pass by a shortened petition after a 40-day wait ([Ridley Law Offices](https://ridleylawoffices.com/small-estate-affidavit-california-2026/)), and Stockton's median sale price was $439,709 in August 2026 ([Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/city/19009/CA/Stockton/housing-market)). Because Cal will not live there, Proposition 19 reassesses the house to market value ([Board of Equalization](https://www.boe.ca.gov/prop19/)). Mail he would be getting: the assessor's change-in-ownership form due within 150 days of death, and an insurer's warning, since many policies "limit coverage once a house sits vacant for 30 or 60 days." A step-up in basis means no tax reason to hurry or wait. The research's own guess suits the character: a son who paid off the mortgage in 2012 probably also had a lawyer put the house in a trust, which leaves him no legal excuse for the seven months.

### Custody

"Week on, week off" is the alternating-weeks schedule: "one complete week with Parent A, then one complete week with Parent B, with the exchange occurring at a consistent time and day, often Sunday evening or Monday morning at school" ([Furubotten Law](https://www.furubottenlaw.com/resources/50-50-custody-schedules-california-complete-guide.html)). It is not the same as 2-2-5-5 and the book should never swap the names. The schedule lives in a court order built on Judicial Council forms FL-341 and its attachments ([California Courts](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/request-for-order/custody-visitation/prepare-agreement)). One-off swaps are informal agreements by text. California's forms impose no rule about new partners; any clause exists only if Jo and Kevin wrote it, so whether she must tell Kevin about Cal is the author's choice.

## Conclusion

The research changes the book's risk profile more than its story. The legal and technical spine was the part most likely to break, and it held: the payoff is possible, irreversible and awkward in exactly the ways the plot needs, and the agent's ask-first design is what the real industry shipped a few months after the story's summer. The exposure is in places with opening hours. A local reader will know that the Palace has no garage to fail to clear, that Abby's class moved in on a Tuesday in mid-August, and that Nepenthe was dark for most of that month.

Reality also handed over material the plan did not have. The semester conversion takes a month of the last summer from a father who was counting on it. The overpayment comes back to Jo as a check in the mail. The gift she never agreed to is recorded on a federal form with her name on it. Movers call the piano move on the porch steps a "cheat." The restaurant where she pays for her own fries closes for a wildfire within days of an early-August lunch. Three checks remain that no desk can do: an hour at the Pied Piper, a conversation with a working mover about who calls the lift, and a lawyer's read of the uninvited payoff.
