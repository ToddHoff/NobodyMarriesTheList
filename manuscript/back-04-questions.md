## For Reading Groups

1. Remy wrote Cal's list in nine seconds, from fourteen years of his life. He agreed with every line. Jo fails eleven of the twelve. What was the list really describing?

2. From line five on, Jo strikes a line only when Cal can tell her why it was there. Which reason surprised you most? What did it tell you about him that the line didn't?

3. The billboard says CALL YOUR MOTHER. When Jo asks Cal whether he called his, he says, "I sent things." Where else in the book does someone send instead of coming? Does Jo ever do it?

4. Janice wrote the messages Jo fell for, from what Cal told her. Does it matter who wrote them if he meant them? Why does Jo take it so hard?

5. Every month for thirteen years, Darlene put the house payment in a shoebox with her son's name on it. What was she waiting for?

6. Jo holds back "I love you" so there's nothing he can buy with it. How is that different from what Cal does with money? How is it the same?

7. Paying off Jo's house is the biggest thing Cal does for her. It nearly ends them. Was it wrong? Would you have been as angry as she was?

8. Jo's rule on every job is that whoever owns the thing is in the room when it gets decided. Where does that rule show up between the two of them?

9. "Every house has a piano nobody put on the estimate." What was Jo's? What was Cal's?

10. Jo adds a thirteenth line: SAYS WHEN HE WAS WRONG. What would yours be? And which line on your own list would you least want to explain?
