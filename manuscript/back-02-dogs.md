## Dogs I Have Met

This is my app. It has every dog I've met, in order. The order is what I say. Here's the top ten.

Owen Silveira, age 10

1. **Pancake.** Golden retriever, the cream kind. Woodside.

He leans on you with his whole side. His head goes to the left since August. That only makes him cuter. He was second for a while. Not anymore.

2. **Daisy.** Basset hound. The library.

She's the reading dog. You read to her and she doesn't care if you get a word wrong. She fell asleep in chapter three. That was the book's fault.

3. **Rocket.** Beagle. The end of our street.

He howls at the garbage truck every Thursday at 7:10. You could set a clock by him.

4. **Tank.** English bulldog. Ryan's house.

He snores louder than Grandpa. He has to be carried up the stairs too.

5. **Pepper.** Border collie. The crosswalk at school.

She comes with the crossing guard. She lets the kindergartners pet her first. That's fair.

6. **Moose.** Newfoundland. Linda Mar Beach.

He was bigger than me. He sat on my foot on purpose.

7. **Peanut.** Mutt. The farmers market.

She has three legs. She's faster than the dogs with four. I checked.

8. **Gus.** Black Lab. The park by our house.

He brings the ball back and then he won't let go of it. That's not how it works, Gus.

9. **Biscuit.** Dachshund. Dad's street.

He barks at the recycling bins. Nobody knows why. I'm working on it.

10. **Lola.** Chihuahua. The laundromat.

She's the smallest dog I've met. She doesn't know.

**Not counted**

Truck Dog. I never met him. It counts for everybody now.

The dog barking in Hilo. I only heard him.
