## Recipes

### Caldo Verde

My father makes it every Sunday it rains. He doesn't measure anything. These are my numbers, from watching him. It takes an hour. It's better the next day.

Makes six bowls.

- 2 pounds russet potatoes, peeled and cut in chunks
- 1 large yellow onion, chopped
- 8 cups water
- 2 teaspoons salt, and more at the end
- 1 big bunch of kale, about 12 ounces
- 1 pound linguiça
- Black pepper

1. Put the potatoes, the onion, the water and the salt in a big pot. Bring it to a boil, then turn it down and let it go till the potatoes give when you push one against the side with a spoon. That's about 20 minutes.

2. While that cooks, do the kale. Strip the leaves off the stems. Stack five or six leaves, roll them up tight, and cut across the roll as thin as you can go, so it comes apart into thread. My father's come out an eighth of an inch wide, every one. Mine don't. It tastes the same.

3. Mash the potatoes right in the pot. Leave a few lumps. My father does.

4. Put the kale in and let it simmer about 5 minutes, till it's soft and still bright green. Gray is too long.

5. Slice the linguiça in rounds and fry them in a pan till the fat comes out, about 5 minutes. Put them in the pot, fat and all.

6. Taste it for salt. Pepper it. Serve it with bread.

If there's a dog in the kitchen, he'll want a round. Blow on it first.

### Linguiça and Egg on a Sweet Roll

*This one is on a card in his printing. He carries blank cards now.*

As Jo makes it. She does not measure. I do.

Makes one, which is two halves.

- 1 link of linguiça, about 4 ounces
- 1 egg
- 1 Portuguese sweet roll

1. Put the linguiça in a pan over medium heat. It will split. The package says it will.

2. Cut it in half lengthwise and put it back in, cut side down. Cook it until the edges go dark, about 10 minutes in all. Take it out. Leave the fat in the pan.

3. Crack the egg into the fat. Cook it until the white is set and the edge is brown. Turn it once, carefully. Do not break the yolk. Jo says it runs into the bread. She is right.

4. Split the roll. Put the egg on the bottom half, the linguiça on the egg and the top half on that.

5. Wrap it in foil and fold the ends twice.

6. At lunch, tear it in half. Hold one half out.

The first one took me twenty-two minutes. I broke the yolk.
