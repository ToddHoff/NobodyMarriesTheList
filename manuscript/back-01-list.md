## The List

It's on my fridge, under the magnet from the yard. He mailed it to me in August, with a line across the top in his blue ink and dates down the side in his pencil. Every line through it is mine.

*I did not write this. I agreed with it. That is worse.*

1. ~~5'8″ or taller~~ *5/7*

He measured me in the bar at the Oak with my own tape. Sixty-two and a quarter. I own six ladders.

2. ~~Age 30 to 36~~ *5/7*

Same table, same night. I asked him whose arithmetic it was. He didn't have an answer.

3. ~~Dresses well~~ *5/16*

The first Saturday at his mother's house. I was in my boots. He said I was the only one of us dressed right.

4. ~~Athletic. Works out most days~~ *5/23*

He goes to a gym four mornings a week. I've never been in one. After one hutch up my ramp, he was the one out of breath.

5. ~~College degree, minimum~~ *6/20*

Two years at Skyline, then the trucks needed somebody. He asked me if I'd wanted to finish. Nobody had asked me that since.

6. ~~Private. No social media~~ *6/6*

I asked if this one was funny. It wasn't. A woman he'd been seeing sold the story of his custody hearing, with Abby's school in it. My name's under four hundred reviews. My cell number's on every box label I print.

7. ~~Comfortable being looked after~~ *I will tell you this one myself.*

He did, at my kitchen table, the night after the last Saturday. He'd sent things all his life. He'd wanted somebody who would want them. I asked him what about now. He said he was in my kitchen.

8. ~~No kids. Wants them~~ *7/25*

The one I was scared of. I'm done having kids. You can't strike that with a pencil. It turned out to be about Abby.

9. ~~Good family~~ *I will tell you this one myself.*

He did, the last Saturday, in his mother's empty living room. He was the lunch lady's son. He'd made sure the other boys never knew she was his.

10. ~~Never married~~ *8/1*

Carmel Beach, with forty dogs and nobody holding a leash. I told him my half of Kevin. He told me how his own marriage ended.

11. ~~Doesn't need to work. Free to travel~~ *I will tell you this one myself.*

I struck it at my table at five in the morning, before he could tell me why. He told me anyway.

12. Says what she wants *I will tell you this one myself.*

He told me on his mother's hearth. She never asked him for one thing, so he quit asking and sent her things. He put it on his list so he wouldn't do that to anybody again. I left this one alone.
